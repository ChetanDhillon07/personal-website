## Overview

Lumina Mobile is a wellness tracking application focused on gentle habit building. The interface relies entirely on typography and spacing to guide the user journey without friction.

## Key Goals

- Focus on minimal sensory load.
- Enhance micro-habits through quiet indicators.
- Emphasize visual breathing room and readability.
